Delete your CuTime account

CuTime lets signed-in customers and barbers submit an account-deletion request from the Android app.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Sign in to the CuTime account you want deleted.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Under Data and privacy, choose Request account deletion.
  4. Review the explanation and choose Submit Request.

If you cannot access the app

Email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the address registered to your CuTime account. Use the subject “CuTime account deletion” and include the account email. Do not send a password or other sensitive authentication information.

What is deleted

After identity verification and any legally required retention review, the account profile and personal data directly associated with the account are deleted or anonymized. This includes authentication access, profile data, device tokens, notification preferences, and barber profile/gallery metadata where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Limited appointment or transaction records may be retained only where required for fraud prevention, dispute handling, security, accounting, or legal obligations. The final retention period must be stated in CuTime's published privacy policy before release.

Release owner action: replace [SUPPORT_EMAIL], confirm the final retention policy, and publish this page at the same HTTPS URL entered in Google Play Console.
