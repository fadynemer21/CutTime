# CuTime demo video script

Target length: 3–5 minutes. Use two fictional accounts and a clean release
candidate build. Prepare the real barber shop before recording.

## Opening — 15 seconds

“CuTime connects customers with real barber shops and gives barbers one place
to manage services, availability, appointments, reviews, photos, and
reminders.”

Show the Welcome screen, then sign in as the Barber account.

## Barber setup — 45 seconds

1. Open Profile and show the public shop name and description.
2. Open Services and show a haircut and haircut-and-beard service.
3. Open Hours, show the weekly schedule, and briefly add/remove a future
   holiday.
4. Show the gallery management entry if Firebase Storage is live.

Explain that only a complete real Barber account appears to customers.

## Customer discovery and booking — 70 seconds

1. Switch to Customer Mode or sign into the Customer account.
2. Search for the shop.
3. Open its profile and point out services, hours, reviews, and gallery.
4. Tap Book Appointment.
5. Select service, valid date, and available time.
6. Emphasize that no order summary appears before Review Booking.
7. Open Review Booking and confirm.
8. Show the new appointment in My Appointments.

Mention that transactions and 15-minute slot locks prevent overlapping
double-booking.

## Cross-role lifecycle — 60 seconds

1. Return to Barber Mode.
2. Show the customer name on the Dashboard appointment.
3. Open appointment details.
4. Explain cancellation/completion ownership.
5. Show holiday cancellation on prepared disposable data, or narrate it without
   changing the main demo appointment.
6. Return to Customer and demonstrate rescheduling.

## Rating and history — 30 seconds

Use a prepared completed appointment:

1. Submit a rating and short review.
2. Show the updated shop rating/review.
3. Show a cancelled appointment and explain long-press history removal.

## Notifications — 25 seconds

Show the notification inbox and settings. State clearly that eligible
appointments schedule both a two-hour and a 30-minute reminder. If push is
deployed, open a notification into appointment details.

## Closing — 15 seconds

“CuTime preserves secure Customer and Barber roles while supporting the full
appointment lifecycle, from discovery to review.”

End on the customer Home or the shop profile. Do not expose passwords, Firebase
screens, tokens, private email addresses, or real personal data.
